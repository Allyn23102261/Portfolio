What I build

Capstone, 2025–26

BantayTubig

A community-based system for tracking red tide outbreaks and fish catch in coastal barangays around Cebu. We're piloting it in Canhabagat, Medellin, working alongside the local government and BFAR to get the reporting flow right.

The hard part isn't the database — it's the interface. Most of the fisherfolk who'll use this have never used an app before, so every screen has to earn its place.

How I work

I got into development through networking, not the other way around — I still think in terms of what talks to what. Before I write a feature, I want to know what breaks it: bad input, a flaky connection, a user who's never seen the screen before.

Most days split between two modes: heads-down build time in the morning, and troubleshooting whatever SuicoIndustries needs fixed in the afternoon. I keep a running list of what I tried and what didn't work — it's saved me from repeating the same dead end twice.
